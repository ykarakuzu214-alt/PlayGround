HOW TO PLAY

THE STORY

Follow Carlos Santana through three chapters. Talk, choose what kind of player he becomes, train, then win the match to move on.

Progress saves automatically after every chapter.

ON THE PITCH

You always control Carlos, marked with a gold ring. Move, chase, pass, and shoot. Teammates and opponents play on their own.

  • WASDARROWS move
  • SPACE hold to charge, release to shoot
  • K pass · tackle when defending
  • L special moves menu
  • ESC pause

1v1 DUELS

When a defender steps up, the game freezes for a command duel. Pick DRIBBLE, PASS, SHOOT or SPECIAL, then stop the sweeping needle in the golden zone.

Timing adds up to +30 to Carlos's stat roll. Specials cost Samba energy.

SAMBA ENERGY

Dribble wins, passes, tackles and goals fill the bar. Spend it on:

  • SAMBA STEP20 — leave a defender behind
  • GOLDEN COMBI35 — one-two into space
  • SANTANA ROTATION55 — a spinning wonder shot

TOUCH & TABLET

Use the virtual stick and the SHOOT, PASS and SPECIAL buttons. Tap dialogue to continue. On a phone, landscape gives you the biggest pitch.

The game works in portrait too. Turn the phone for widescreen.

WANT TO WIN?

Shoot with the sweet spot: charge until the ring turns gold, aim with up/down, and release. Low shots into the corners beat most keepers.

A draw goes to golden goal, then penalties. The story waits for a win.
