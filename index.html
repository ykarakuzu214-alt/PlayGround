HOW TO PLAY

THE MATCH

Carlos's team attacks upward. When you have the ball, pick DRIBBLE, PASS, SHOOT, a SPECIAL move, or HOLD to catch your breath.

When the enemy has the ball, choose a defensive posture: PRESS to fight for it, BLOCK to guard a shot, or FALL BACK to recover stamina.

THE METER

Every duel opens a timing meter. Stop the marker in the green zone to boost your power. A perfect stop can beat a stronger opponent.

Keyboard: Arrow keys / W A S D to pick, Enter or Space to confirm, Space to stop the meter.
Touch: tap the buttons.

Shots only work from the attacking half. Specials cost stamina. At half time, both teams recover.
